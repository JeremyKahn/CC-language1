Getting started

  1. Open ⚙️ Settings and paste your Anthropic API key (required) and, ideally, an OpenAI API key for high-quality speech.
  2. Add a few words you know to Vocabulary → Mastered, and words you're working on to Learning (or just start practising — the app builds your lists as you go).
  3. Try Listen & Repeat to train listening and pronunciation, Reading for custom texts, and Grammar for a structured course.

Listen & Repeat

Difficulty level: 1 / 10
…
The tutor says a phrase and the microphone opens automatically — just repeat it; recording stops by itself when you finish speaking. A near-perfect repeat (≥92%) raises the difficulty; a decent one (≥75%) moves on at the same level. If you miss, the phrase is repeated slowly; if you miss again, you get a simpler one.
